Our Services

Administrative support that keeps your practice running

Pick exactly what you need. We take care of the back office while you take care of patients.

Medical Coding

Accurate ICD-10-CM, CPT® and HCPCS Level II coding based on your documentation, helping reduce denials and keep claims compliant.

Medical Billing

Charge entry, claim preparation and submission handled end to end, so your claims go out clean and on time.

Denial Management & A/R Follow-up

We track unpaid and denied claims, identify the root cause, correct and resubmit — and work aging A/R so revenue doesn’t slip away.

Insurance Verification

Eligibility and benefits checks before the visit, reducing surprises for your patients and rejections for your practice.

Provider Credentialing

Payer enrollment and re-credentialing support, keeping paperwork and deadlines organized so you can see patients without delays.

Coding Audits & Documentation Review

Chart reviews that highlight coding gaps and documentation opportunities, with clear, practical feedback for your team.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of practices do you work with?

We support clinics and healthcare providers of different sizes — from solo physicians to multi-provider practices. Reach out and tell us about your specialty so we can confirm the right fit.

How do you protect patient information?

We follow HIPAA-aligned practices, including minimum-necessary access, secure communication channels and signing a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with our clients.

Do I need to change my EHR or practice management system?

No. We work within the systems you already use, so your team doesn’t need to learn new software.

Can I use just one service, like coding only?

Yes. You can choose a single service or combine several. We build the engagement around what your practice needs.

How do we get started?

Send us a message through the contact form or email us directly. We’ll schedule a free consultation to understand your needs.
